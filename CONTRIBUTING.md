# 参与方式

Agentic Payment Signal 欢迎三类贡献：

1. 推荐信息源
2. 推荐具体 signal
3. 反馈分类、筛选标准或样例格式

## 推荐信息源

请尽量提供：

- source 名称
- 官方链接
- 它覆盖什么主题
- 为什么它和 agentic payment 有关
- 它是官方来源、媒体、研究机构、监管来源，还是社区来源

推荐格式：

```md
Source: <name>
URL: <link>
Type: official / media / research / regulatory / community
Why it matters:
What to watch:
```

## 推荐具体 signal

请尽量提供：

- 原始链接
- 发生了什么
- 为什么它和 AI × crypto × payment 有关
- 适合谁看
- 你认为后续应该观察什么

推荐格式：

```md
Title:
URL:
What changed:
Why it matters:
Audience:
Tags:
Watch next:
```

## 什么内容不适合提交

- 只关于币价或短线交易
- airdrop、meme、KOL 喊单
- 没有原始来源的二手消息
- 与 payment、wallet、stablecoin、identity、risk、compliance 无关的泛 AI / Web3 内容

## 提交方式

第一阶段可以通过 GitHub Issue 或 Pull Request 提交。请尽量保持内容简洁、可核查、有原始来源。

# Contributing

Agentic Payment Signal welcomes three types of contributions:

1. Source suggestions
2. Specific signal suggestions
3. Feedback on categories, screening criteria, or sample format

## Suggest a Source

Please include:

- source name
- official URL
- what it covers
- why it matters for agentic payment
- whether it is an official source, media source, research source, regulatory source, or community source

Suggested format:

```md
Source: <name>
URL: <link>
Type: official / media / research / regulatory / community
Why it matters:
What to watch:
```

## Suggest a Signal

Please include:

- original link
- what changed
- why it matters for AI × crypto × payment
- who should care
- what to watch next

Suggested format:

```md
Title:
URL:
What changed:
Why it matters:
Audience:
Tags:
Watch next:
```

## What Not to Submit

- Token price or short-term trading content
- Airdrops, meme cycles, or KOL calls
- Second-hand claims without an original source
- Generic AI / Web3 content unrelated to payments, wallets, stablecoins, identity, risk, or compliance

## How to Submit

In the first phase, use GitHub Issues or Pull Requests. Keep submissions concise, verifiable, and linked to original sources whenever possible.
