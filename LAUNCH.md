## What This Is

This is the public signal system I use to track the middle layer of Agentic Payment.

It watches how AI agents, stablecoin rails, wallets, payment APIs, identity, risk, compliance, and merchant networks are slowly converging into machine-executable payment infrastructure.

The goal is not to cover every piece of AI x crypto news. The goal is to track signals that make Agentic Payment more real.

## Why Now

The end state of Agentic Payment is easy to describe: AI agents can make purchases, pay for services, procure APIs, manage budgets, and transact with other agents inside user-defined permissions.

The hard part is the path between human confirmation and machine-executed payment.

That path needs:

- authorization
- identity
- wallet permissions
- stablecoin settlement
- payment APIs
- merchant trust
- fraud and compliance controls
- responsibility boundaries

This project tracks the signals that show those layers emerging.
