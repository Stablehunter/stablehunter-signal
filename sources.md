## 补充来源原则

推荐新 source 时，请优先说明：

- 它是否是官方来源或一手数据
- 它是否长期覆盖 payment、stablecoin、wallet、identity、risk 或 compliance
- 它是否能产生可行动的 builder signal，而不只是新闻噪音
- 它是否适合被稳定监控，而不是偶然看一眼

## Source Suggestion Principles

When suggesting a new source, explain:

- Whether it is official or close to primary data
- Whether it consistently covers payments, stablecoins, wallets, identity, risk, or compliance
- Whether it produces actionable builder signals rather than generic news noise
- Whether it is suitable for recurring monitoring
